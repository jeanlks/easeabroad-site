Delete your EaseAbroad account

EaseAbroad · Contact: support@easeabroad.app

You can delete your EaseAbroad account and its data at any time, from the app or by email.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open EaseAbroad and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email

If you can't use the app, email support@easeabroad.app with the subject Delete my account.

Send it from the email address you use for your EaseAbroad account, so we can verify that the request comes from the account owner. We'll delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm (usually much sooner).

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address and password hash.
  • Your trips and itineraries, places you added, and trip-sharing entries.
  • Your travel preferences, language and app settings.
  • Your subscription record and daily usage counters.

When

This data is deleted from our live database immediately when you delete the account in the app, or when we process your email request. Server logs and backups that may still contain it expire automatically within 30 days. Tour progress and settings stored on your device are removed when you uninstall the app.

What is not deleted

  • Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings (Subscriptions), otherwise the store may keep billing you. Google and Apple keep their own purchase records under their privacy policies.
  • Public place information (cached data from OpenStreetMap and Wikipedia) is not linked to you and is not personal data.

See our Privacy Policy for more details.
